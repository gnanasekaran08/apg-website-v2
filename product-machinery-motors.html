Industrial Motors & Heavy Machinery Salvage

Industrial electric motors, HVAC compressors, generators, and distribution transformers contain significant amounts of high-purity copper windings, heavy steel casings, and valuable cast iron cores. APG Recycling provides comprehensive purchasing, dismantling, and transport services for all types of industrial motors and machinery scrap across Singapore.

Whether you are upgrading factory equipment, clearing out a ship engine room, or dismantling a commercial HVAC system, our fleet of crane trucks and heavy lifting gear handles on-site loading directly at your facility or worksite.

What Determines Machinery Scrap Pricing?

Machinery scrap prices are evaluated based on total weight, copper-to-iron ratio, motor horsepower rating, and whether the unit is intact or dismantled. Complete intact units are bought at solid per-ton or per-kg rates, while pre-dismantled copper windings receive premium non-ferrous rates.
