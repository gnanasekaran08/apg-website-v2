A Decade of Excellence in Singapore's Scrap & Metal Salvage Market

APG Recycling Pte. Ltd. (UEN: 201436195N) was incorporated on December 4, 2014, in Singapore. Strategically headquartered at No. 1 Soon Lee Street, #01-11 Pioneer Centre, Singapore 627605 in the heart of Jurong Industrial Estate, APG Recycling has established itself as one of the region's most reputable merchants for scrap metal trading, factory clearance, and industrial salvage.

Our company operates a fully equipped primary warehouse facility specializing in the wholesale purchasing, grading, sorting, and processing of non-ferrous metals (copper, brass, aluminum, stainless steel) and heavy ferrous structural steel. In addition, APG provides turnkey site demolition recovery, heavy machinery transport, and road plate rental services for construction contractors across Singapore.
