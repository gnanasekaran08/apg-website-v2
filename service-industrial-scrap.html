Streamlined Factory Scrap Disposal

Manufacturing facilities, engineering workshops, and industrial plants generate substantial quantities of metal offcuts, turnings, and stampings daily. Unmanaged industrial scrap creates workplace hazards, consumes valuable floor space, and leads to unaccounted revenue loss.

APG Recycling provides complete end-to-end industrial scrap management programs. We place custom-built scrap collection containers directly at your facility, establish routine collection schedules, and handle logistics with our hook-lift truck fleet.

Custom Container Bins

Placement of 1.5-ton skip bins to 20-ton heavy-duty hook-lift containers tailored to your factory layout and volume.

Scheduled Next-Day Pickup

Guaranteed next-day or emergency 24/7 container swaps across Jurong, Tuas, Pioneer, Woodlands, and Changi.

Transparent Weight Records

Calibrated digital scale indicators print official weighbridge tickets for instant verification and auditing.

ESG Carbon Audit Reports

Monthly sustainability documentation calculating carbon offset and landfill diversion metrics for corporate ESG reporting.
