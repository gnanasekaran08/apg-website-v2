Stainless Steel Scrap (304 & 316 Grade)

Clean Grade 304 and high-molybdenum Grade 316 stainless steel pipes, industrial tanks, marine fittings, food processing equipment, and sheet turnings.

High-Purity Stainless Steel Scrap Purchasing

Stainless steel is a highly valuable alloy valued for its nickel and chromium content. At APG Recycling, we accept all forms of non-magnetic and low-magnetic stainless steel scrap, including food-grade 304 alloys and marine-grade 316 alloys containing molybdenum for corrosion resistance.

We utilize XRF handheld analyzers at our Pioneer yard to accurately test nickel and molybdenum percentage on-the-spot, guaranteeing you receive true market value based on precise metallurgical purity.

Key Value Drivers for Stainless Scrap

Grade 316 commands a premium over Grade 304 due to its 2% molybdenum content. Clean, solid stainless scrap free of mild steel attachments or iron contamination earns top dollar. Machine turnings and contaminated solids are graded based on oil content and cleanliness.
