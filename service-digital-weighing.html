100% Honest & Calibrated Weighing

Trust and transparency are the foundational pillars of APG Recycling. We recognize that clients require total confidence when selling high-value non-ferrous metals like copper, brass, and aluminum.

All scrap metal brought to our Pioneer Centre warehouse (#01-11, No 1 Soon Lee Street) or processed on-site is weighed using high-precision digital scale indicators. Customers view the digital readout display directly in real time and receive printed weighbridge receipts.
