Heavy Ferrous Steel Scrap

Plates, structural I-beams, columns, anchor chains, marine vessel scrap, and heavy industrial iron processed to ISRI commodity specifications.

Material Overview & Specifications

Ferrous scrap constitutes the largest volume of recycled metals worldwide. APG Recycling purchases all grades of heavy iron and steel scrap generated from demolition sites, manufacturing plants, shipyard vessel dismantling, and civil engineering projects across Singapore.

All materials are sorted, cut, sheared, and prepared to internationally recognized ISRI commodity specifications (such as HMS 1, HMS 2, P&S Plate & Structural) for direct consumption by steel mills and foundries.

Structural Steel Plates & Beams

Thick mild steel plates, H-beams, universal columns, steel channels, and I-section girders from building frames.

Anchor Chains & Marine Salvage

Heavy forged anchor chain links, metal buoys, barge hulls, vessel deck plates, and shipyard scrap.

Rebar & Wire Rope Scrap

Construction reinforced steel bar (rebar), high-tensile wire rope, and crane hoisting cables.

Factory Steel Turnings & Shavings

CNC machining steel turnings, metal stampings, punchings, and industrial lathe scrap.
